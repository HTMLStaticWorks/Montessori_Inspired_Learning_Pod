Our Story & Pedagogy · Est. 2012

Nurturing authentic independence & lifelong curiosity.

Little Oak Montessori was founded on Dr. Maria Montessori's foundational principle: follow the child. For over 14 years, our prepared environments have nurtured self-directed discovery, deep concentration, and emotional resilience.

The child who has felt a strong love for his surroundings and for all living creatures, who has discovered joy and enthusiasm in work, gives us reason to hope.

Educational Philosophy

The three pillars of Little Oak.

Guided by Association Montessori Internationale (AMI) principles, our pod nurtures intrinsic curiosity and lifelong independence.

🌱

Self-Directed Learning

Children choose purposeful work from structured Montessori learning areas, building deep focus without artificial rewards or pressures.

🤝

Mixed-Age Communities

Multi-age classrooms empower older children to mentor peers while younger children naturally absorb higher-level social and academic skills.

🏛️

Prepared Environments

Ergonomic child-scaled furniture, self-correcting wooden materials, and open spaces designed to foster autonomy and order.

Our Mission & Vision

Nurturing the whole child through scientific observation, intrinsic motivation, and respectful community.

🎯

Our Mission

To cultivate each child's intrinsic curiosity, self-discipline, and deep joy of learning within an authentic prepared Montessori environment.

  • Follow each child's individual sensitive periods and readiness
  • Foster compassionate collaboration over competitive pressure
  • Instill lifelong practical independence and ecological care
🔭

Our Vision

A world where young people grow into self-reliant, empathetic, and courageous thinkers who shape peaceful, sustainable communities.

  • Build unshakeable confidence in personal creative agency
  • Inspire deep reverence for living systems and global culture
  • Cultivate thoughtful leaders grounded in emotional literacy
Our Journey & Pedagogy

A warm, home-centered pod dedicated to excellence.

What started as a small neighborhood learning pod has grown into a vibrant Montessori community. We combine rigorous pedagogical observation with a cozy, family-centered atmosphere.

  • 3-hour uninterrupted morning work cycles
  • 1:8 Guide-to-student ratio for individual observation
  • Integrated practical life, sensorial, language & math
  • Transparent school-family alliance & regular progress updates

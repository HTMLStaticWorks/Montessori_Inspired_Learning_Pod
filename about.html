Our History & Pedagogy

Nurturing authentic independence since 2012.

Little Oak Montessori was founded on Dr. Maria Montessori's core principle: follow the child. We provide prepared indoor and outdoor environments that foster self-directed learning, deep concentration, and emotional resilience.

Educational Philosophy

The three pillars of Little Oak.

Guided by Association Montessori Internationale (AMI) principles, our pod nurtures intrinsic curiosity and lifelong independence.

🌱

Self-Directed Learning

Children choose purposeful work from structured Montessori learning areas, building deep focus without artificial rewards or pressures.

🤝

Mixed-Age Communities

Multi-age classrooms empower older children to mentor peers while younger children naturally absorb higher-level social and academic skills.

🏛️

Prepared Environments

Ergonomic child-scaled furniture, self-correcting wooden materials, and open spaces designed to foster autonomy and order.

Our Journey & Pedagogy

A warm, home-centered pod dedicated to excellence.

What started as a small neighborhood learning pod has grown into a vibrant Montessori community. We combine rigorous pedagogical observation with a cozy, family-centered atmosphere.

  • 3-hour uninterrupted morning work cycles
  • 1:8 Guide-to-student ratio for individual observation
  • Integrated practical life, sensorial, language & math
  • Transparent school-family alliance & regular progress updates

4 Simple Steps

The enrollment journey.

Our thoughtful admissions process ensures a harmonious fit between family expectations and Montessori philosophy.

1. Online Inquiry

Complete our quick digital application sharing your child's age, developmental background, and desired start term.

2. Morning Observation

Attend a guided 45-minute classroom observation to watch active work cycles and ask questions to our guides.

3. Family Conversation

An informal meeting between parents and our Pedagogy Director to discuss child readiness and school-home alignment.

4. Welcome & Orientation

Receive your admission letter, complete student registration, and attend our gentle child-parent orientation day.

Cohort Readiness

Eligibility & Age Groups

We welcome children according to developmental planes, sensitive readiness, and social readiness.

1.5 – 3 Years

Toddler Nest Eligibility

For children beginning their journey outside the home with gross and fine motor curiosity.

  • Independent walking and movement stability
  • Showing early desire for self-feeding
  • Gentle collaborative toilet learning readiness
  • Comfort with small peer group settings
3 – 6 Years

Casa Primary Eligibility

The cornerstone 3-year Montessori cycle requiring sustained focus and community grace.

  • Complete independent bathroom autonomy
  • Ability to follow 2 to 3 step verbal directions
  • Readiness for 3-hour morning work blocks
  • Respect for shared materials and peers
6 – 12 Years

Elementary Pod Eligibility

For collaborative thinkers entering the second plane of development and cosmic education.

  • Foundational phonetic reading & basic math
  • Capacity for cooperative peer projects
  • Prior Montessori experience or readiness review
  • Curiosity for science, history & cosmic research

Frequently Asked Questions

Common admissions questions.

Answers to popular parent questions regarding readiness, daily meals, and program schedules.

Must be trained about Toilet?

Toilet independence is required for our Casa Primary program (age 3+). For our Toddler Community (1.5–3), we work collaboratively with families on gentle toilet learning.

Age-based toilet learning guidelines

Are meals & snacks provided?

Yes. Organic, wholesome snacks and family-style meals are integrated directly into our Practical Life curriculum where children participate in table prep and cleanup.

Organic snacks & family dining

What are the program hours?

Toddler & Half-Day Casa run 8:45 AM – 12:30 PM. Full-Day Casa & Elementary run 8:45 AM – 3:00 PM with optional extended aftercare until 5:30 PM.

Campus Visit Guidelines

Planning your tour.

We believe visiting an active classroom during morning work cycle is the single best way to experience Montessori education.

  • Morning tours held Tuesdays & Thursdays at 9:15 AM
  • Adults-only initial observation for quiet classroom environment
  • Student visit & interaction arranged during step 3 of admissions
  • Free designated visitor parking available at main gate

Curriculum Journey

The 5 core Montessori learning areas.

Self-correcting wooden materials transform abstract concepts into tangible experiences children can touch, explore, and master independently.

  • Practical Life: Fine motor control, care of self & care of environment
  • Sensorial: Refinement of sight, touch, pitch, spatial perception & dimension
  • Language Arts: Sandpaper letters, movable alphabet & story composition
  • Mathematics: Decimal system, bead chains, fraction circles & multiplication
  • Cultural Studies: Geography puzzle maps, botany, zoology & timeline of life
